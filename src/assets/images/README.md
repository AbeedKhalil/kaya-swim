# Photos — drop-in slots

Save a photo here under one of these names and it appears on the site automatically.
Any of `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.png`, `.webp` or `.avif` works; the build converts it to
fast AVIF/WebP in several sizes. Empty slots show water art instead.

| File name (any extension)  | Where it appears                          | Shape | Recommended size |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- | ----- | ---------------- |
| `founder-portrait`         | Founder story + small round hero avatar   | 4:5   | 1200 × 1500      |
| `track-water-experience`   | Water Experience track page               | 4:3   | 1600 × 1200      |
| `track-excellence`         | Swimming Excellence track page            | 4:3   | 1600 × 1200      |
| `track-fear-of-water`      | Fear-of-water track page                  | 4:3   | 1600 × 1200      |
| `pool-givat-ela`           | Location section                          | 3:2   | 1600 × 1067      |

Tips: use well-lit photos, keep faces away from the edges (photos are cropped to the
shape above), and only publish photos of children with their parents' permission.
